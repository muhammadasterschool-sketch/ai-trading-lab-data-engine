## 2. Claim Categories — STRICTLY SEPARATED (mandate §26)

### 2.1 Contract verification — what the tests prove

The 1,059-test suite proves, deterministically and repeatedly
(3 consecutive full runs, 17.5–17.9 s each):

- PIT correctness: future bars invisible at cutoffs; revision
  first-arrival-wins; permutation invariance; exact ±1 µs boundary
  behavior; timezone representation cannot move a cutoff.
- Label/feature boundary: overlap raises (fail closed), by
  construction and by proof.
- Identity determinism: no wall clock / RNG / PID anywhere in
  identity paths; cross-run and cross-process stability inherited
  from the Phase 4 hashing contract.
- Fail-closed discipline: all 14 no-prediction gate conditions block
  with machine-readable reasons; DRIFTED/INVALID drift states can
  never continue as valid production states.
- Tamper evidence: ledger chain verification fails on any mutation;
  provenance/output hash changes on any field swap.
- The benchmark harness itself: temporal partitions with purge +
  embargo, split manifests, per-split event evaluation, Platt fitted
  on validation only, refusal tallies, per-feature PSI — all
  deterministic and hash-stable.

### 2.4 Unsupported claims — what must NOT be claimed

Until a REAL_VERIFIED dataset (≥ 5 verified years, human-approved
source, passed quality gates) exists and an empirical benchmark run
is produced from it, NONE of the following may be claimed:

- "the system predicts crashes accurately"
- "the model beats the market / reliably predicts crashes"
- "the model is production ready / profitable / prevents crashes"
- "the system has proven predictive power"
- any empirical calibration validity, detection rate, lead time,
  false-alarm rate, or regime-conditioned performance number
